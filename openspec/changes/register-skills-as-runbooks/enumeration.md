## Post-change truth this enumeration is written against

All six skills (recall, learn, please, route, curate, write-memory) ship again as skills in
`agent-instructions/skills/` via `engram update`. Each MAY have one vault runbook note (basename
slug `skill-<name>`, frontmatter `skill_hash`) whose body mirrors the SKILL.md and whose
situation/triggers/done_when/red_flags are authored on the note. `engram update` and
`engram register-skills` (`--accept`/`--decline`/`--dry-run`/`--adopt`) offer to
register/refresh/remove those notes; declines are remembered by hash in vault-root
`skill-registrations.json`; nothing is written without a terminal. New/refreshed notes are
pending offers the `curate` skill authors/re-checks and clears. `recall`/`learn` invoke
`write-memory` **natively as a skill** again (not by basename/wikilink fetch). `please` is one
note (its former sub-notes 1042/1043/1044 removed, merged into the single note/skill body);
`recall`'s glance/deep split is not carried forward. `engram show` falls back to
`ENGRAM_PARENT` on a local miss, labeling output `# from_parent: true`. The runbook concept
itself (ADR-0026, captured runbooks, triggers, the shim follow-frame) is unchanged — only the
"skills are replaced by runbooks" direction is reversed.

**Historical vs. forward-looking rule applied throughout:** a dated record of what already
happened (an ADR's original Decision/Consequences text, a ROADMAP "Shipped" row's account of a
past ship) is never rewritten — it gets an appended, dated amendment/note instead. A
present-tense claim (what the system does now) or a forward-looking claim (what a NOW-band
roadmap item proposes to do next, still assuming the now-reversed direction) is rewritten
outright ("now"). A spec's Purpose line can never be touched by a delta spec (an OpenSpec
constraint), so a stale Purpose is always "archive-time" regardless of how present-tense its
wording reads.
