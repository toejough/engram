## 855b14 A slow step is waited for, a stalled one is not

With no time bound before a [prompt](../793_automatic-memory/793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms) reaches the [agent](../793_automatic-memory/793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms), a step that is only slow is waited for ([855a3c 793a2d Never in the way](./855a_product-spec.md#855a3c-modified-793a2d-never-in-the-way)). A step that never answers cannot hold the prompt forever, and the host puts no ceiling on it: its type definitions (`claude-code.d.ts`, bundled with Claude Code 2.1.301 under `/private/tmp/claude-501/bundled-skills/2.1.301/e2ebd773aa00aeae2764ffb5641f2a63/plugin-authoring/types/`) give a hook a budget of `10_000` milliseconds (line 5250) but say "Each bounds the hook's OWN time: the clock stops while a `next(e)` call or any `$` call of the hook's is in flight" (line 5234), `timeoutMs` of a model call has "default none" (line 6365), and a command runs 30 seconds by default, ten minutes at most (line 3592). So the bound is a liveness bound of 2 minutes, far above any normal judgement: a judgement or delivery with no answer by then is stepped aside, delivers nothing and is shown as a failure. It is a liveness bound and not a latency target. Rejected: holding until the user interrupts; a host ceiling, which does not exist for these calls. Decided by: Owner (2026-10-18), option (b) of [855c1 How long may a judgement or delivery run before it is given up?](./855c_open-questions.md#855c1-how-long-may-a-judgement-or-delivery-run-before-it-is-given-up).

## 855b27 Each wait has its own 2 minutes

The reply of the candidate judgement is `{"candidates":[{"id","verdict","newer"}]}`, and at deep depth each entry may add `chunks` and `fields`, and `lessons` stands beside `candidates`: the keys [855d4c 793d4a Delivering the notes that bear on a prompt](./855d_technical-spec.md#855d4c-modified-793d4a-delivering-the-notes-that-bear-on-a-prompt) and [855d4j Writing what a deep delivery writes](./855d_technical-spec.md#855d4j-added-writing-what-a-deep-delivery-writes) read. Rejected: the window as a list of lines, which the mod would have to join. Only the request is redacted, as the owner decided on 2026-10-18: the carry holds the candidates raw, since the delivery shows the [agent](../793_automatic-memory/793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms) the same [notes](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) raw through `engram show`. Alternative: redact the text and the situation in the carry and the block, notes and passages alike, as this decision first had it. Decided by: Author (default; owner may override).

## 855b56 The date of a note is its created date, and the date of a chunk the time the index holds

A candidate line carries a date so that the model can tell which of two conflicting candidates is newer ([855b32 Candidates are judged one by one, and the session's own transcript is not a candidate](./855b_decision-log.md#855b32-candidates-are-judged-one-by-one-and-the-sessions-own-transcript-is-not-a-candidate)). No field of a [note](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) records an amendment, so the date of a note is its `created` field, and an amended note looks as old as it was written. The date of a chunk is the time its index record holds, `undated` for a chunk indexed before the index kept the time. [`engram ingest`](../../GLOSSARY.md#engram-ingest-subcommand) stamps every chunk of a [transcript](../../GLOSSARY.md#transcript) with the time of the last row of its [session](../../GLOSSARY.md#session) (`ingestTimeFor`), so a catch-up ingest does not make it today; a markdown chunk carries the time it was ingested, and a chunk of a transcript whose rows hold no time, the time of the ingest. The row time of each chunk would change the chunker and the index schema, which belong to another component. Rejected: the date of the file, which the lookup does not return, and a stamp written by `engram amend`, which changes another component ([855c6 Which date does a note carry?](./855c_open-questions.md#855c6-which-date-does-a-note-carry)). Decided by: Author (default; owner may override), question 855c6, answered by default.

## 855b59 The cap keeps a floor of passages, and the budget ends the list at the first that does not fit

The lookup is asked for 60 items, twice the most candidates, so that the floor has passages to keep when the [notes](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) score higher. The candidates are in the order of their scores, notes and chunks together. At most 30 are kept, and when passages matched, at least the smaller of 10 and the passages matched are passages and the notes fill the rest: the idea of [792b24c6 The matched-note floor](../../specs/technical/query-engine.md#792b24c6-the-matched-note-floor), for passages. The 60,000 characters (counted in characters of the text of a candidate after the redaction and the cut) are a prefix of that list: the first candidate that would pass them ends the list, and every later one goes, so that a shorter candidate behind a long one does not jump the order ([855d8b The candidates of the lookup](./855d_technical-spec.md#855d8b-the-candidates-of-the-lookup)). A [note](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) of more than 60,000 characters alone leaves no candidate, and the command prints nothing. Measured on a synthetic [vault](../../GLOSSARY.md#vault) of 1,200 notes and 1,121 chunks, with the 12 phrases of the earlier measurement: at glance, 30 candidates, 3 of them passages (all that the 60 items held); at deep, 30 notes and no passage, because the synthetic chunks score below the notes, so the floor has none to keep there; the property of [855d4b1m A cap on the count keeps a floor of passages](./855d_technical-spec.md#855d4b1-scenarios) proves the floor for any mixed set. Rejected: skipping the candidate that does not fit and going on; the order of the payload, which lists the notes first and so drops every chunk at the cap. Decided by: Author (default; owner may override), question [855c7 Does the cap on the candidates keep a floor of passages?](./855c_open-questions.md#855c7-does-the-cap-on-the-candidates-keep-a-floor-of-passages).

## 855b92 A deep delivery writes once its delivery succeeded, even when the block is empty

The mod runs `write` from a timer when the recall printed `"write":true` and the delivery exited with 0, whether or not the block holds a line: the lessons the evidence shows and the [notes](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) the evidence revises do not depend on a line being left after the lines the [session](../../GLOSSARY.md#session) holds are dropped. A model that gives no text, a failed step and a delivery that fails run no write, only the report of the failure ([855d2a1 Scenarios](./855d_technical-spec.md#855d2a1-scenarios)). The input is the session's ID, the cue, the reply and the carry, and the write is given 60 seconds; a write that fails is reported with a failure that begins `the write after a deep delivery failed`. Alternative: write only when a block was given, which loses a lesson for a [prompt](../793_automatic-memory/793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms) whose notes the session already holds. Decided by: Author (default; owner may override).

## 855b93 The reply of a deep recall is read entry by entry, and the forms of a field are those the instructions show

A candidate entry that is not an object of strings is left out with a reason and does not lose the others; a lesson is read as [855d4i 793d4h Writing the lessons of a reply](./855d_technical-spec.md#855d4i-modified-793d4h-writing-the-lessons-of-a-reply) reads it, facts and feedback only (a runbook is recorded as skipped), with `chunks` and a `supersedes` that is the file name of a [note](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) whose `type` and `claim` the lesson holds, as the instructions of the deep recall show, or an object or a list of objects that hold their own. The fields a near note is revised by are `situation` and those of its kind (fact: subject, predicate, object; feedback: behavior, impact, action; runbook: done_when, body); a field it has no such field as is recorded as skipped with the note and the field, and a blank value is dropped without a record. A covered note with no passage of the carry among its chunks, and a near note with neither a passage nor a field left, are not amended and leave no record. Alternative: fail the reply on the first entry that is not whole, which loses the whole write. Decided by: Author (default; owner may override).
