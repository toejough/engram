## 793b100 The sweep indexes the session's record after every turn

The sweep indexes the [session](../../GLOSSARY.md#session)'s record as `--transcript` of [`engram ingest`](../../GLOSSARY.md#engram-ingest-subcommand) does ([793d4n Sweeping the conversation](./793d_technical-spec.md#793d4n-sweeping-the-conversation)), after every [turn](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms), and not the roots of `--auto`: the promise is the [session](../../GLOSSARY.md#session)'s conversation, an explicit source indexes a session in a throwaway directory where `--auto` leaves it out, and the cost is one session. Measured on a copy of the owner's 556 sessions (223 MB): 0.08 s when nothing changed and 1.7 s after one turn of a session of 26 MB (625 chunks), against 1,447 s for a first sweep of all 556; a first sweep of the session of 26 MB took 264 s (0.42 s a chunk), which is [793c70 Is a sweep that is ended at its bound retried at every turn?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c70-is-a-sweep-that-is-ended-at-its-bound-retried-at-every-turn). Rejected: `--auto` (it rescans every root and takes the backlog of old sessions that [793b8 Exclusions](#793b8-exclusions) leaves out) and a sweep less often than every turn (it is cheap). A ([793c68 Does the sweep index the session's record only?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c68-does-the-sweep-index-the-sessions-record-only)).

## 793b101 A second sweep waits for the lock of the ingest

[`engram ingest`](../../GLOSSARY.md#engram-ingest-subcommand) holds an exclusive lock on the manifest from before it reads the manifest until after it writes it (#660, [792b18c2 Locked reconciliation](../../specs/technical/chunk-index-store.md#792b18c2-locked-reconciliation)), so two sweeps, and a sweep and the [agent](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms)'s closing sweep, are serialized in Go with nothing added. A killed sweep releases the lock with its process and leaves the index files and the manifest as they were, since they are written whole, by rename ([793d4n1e A sweep that is ended leaves the chunk index usable](./793d_technical-spec.md#793d4n1-scenarios)). The sweep waits within its bound: one skipped while another runs would leave the [turn](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms)'s conversation unsearchable until the next sweep, against [793a2f The conversation becomes searchable memory](./793a_product-spec.md#793a2f-the-conversation-becomes-searchable-memory). Rejected: a skip with a recorded reason when one runs (it needs a non-blocking lock in the ingest). A ([793c69 Should a second sweep wait for the lock or be skipped?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c69-should-a-second-sweep-wait-for-the-lock-or-be-skipped)).

## 793b102 The sweep and the capture run apart

The mod starts the sweep and the capture of a [turn](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms) together, each with a bound of its own ([793d2c Conversation sweep flow](./793d_technical-spec.md#793d2c-conversation-sweep-flow)): the sweep indexes the [session](../../GLOSSARY.md#session)'s record, where no [note](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) enters, and the capture writes notes, so neither reads what the other writes, no order is needed and neither extends the other's bound. A headless turn is swept only when it is judged ([793d4m Telling the prompts the user sends](./793d_technical-spec.md#793d4m-telling-the-prompts-the-user-sends)), so that a script run with `claude -p` indexes nothing by default. A ([793c78 Do the sweep and the capture of a turn run apart?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c78-do-the-sweep-and-the-capture-of-a-turn-run-apart)).

## 793b103 The work after a turn is started from a timer

The host holds the next event of a mod, and so the next [prompt](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms), until the calls of the host that the previous event left in flight settle: a sweep of 60 seconds, started from `turn.complete` with no await, kept the next prompt out of the [session](../../GLOSSARY.md#session) for 60 seconds, and the wait measured from the row of the prompt in the record missed it. A call made from a timer of the mod (`$.clock.after`) holds nothing, so the capture and the sweep start from one, and the wait of a prompt is measured from the moment it is sent. Rejected: a loop on `$.process.spawn` (it held the reply of the next [turn](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms) for 21 seconds) and the calls inside the hook, as built before (the prompt waited for them). A ([793c71 How is the work after a turn kept from holding the next prompt?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c71-how-is-the-work-after-a-turn-kept-from-holding-the-next-prompt)).

## 793b105 What is shown, and how

The lines that show the user what [automatic memory](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms) did go through the host's log (`$.ui.log`), not through the [prompt](./793a_product-spec.md#793a1a-terms)'s context: the log appends one line to the conversation as the user sees it, drawn like a system notice and not sent to the model, and a host that streams JSON receives it as a `ui_log` event (verified on Claude Code 2.1.302, from a hook and from a timer). The context is what the model reads beside the prompt and the user never sees, so the lines put there were seen by the model only, which they also told of every failure, against [793a2d Never in the way](./793a_product-spec.md#793a2d-never-in-the-way). The block of the delivery carries the [notes](../../GLOSSARY.md#permanent-note) only; [793d4p Telling what automatic memory did](./793d_technical-spec.md#793d4p-telling-what-automatic-memory-did) prints the lines on standard error and the mod shows each ([793d2a Prompt delivery flow](./793d_technical-spec.md#793d2a-prompt-delivery-flow)). A line is shown for each note delivered, when it is delivered, and for each record of what was written, left out or failed, at the first prompt after the record was made. A line is shown once: the delivery marks each record whose line it printed by a file beside the record, because the host keeps a log line in no record of the [session](../../GLOSSARY.md#session), where the lines of the block were read back from. At most 5 lines of records are shown at a prompt, and a reason is one line, redacted and cut in its middle, because the cause of an error is at its end. The scenarios `793d4c1d` and `793d4d1g` are retired with the lines in the block. Rejected: the block (the user never sees it), a cursor in the mod (it shows the history of a resumed session again, or puts state in the mod) and the lines read back from the session's record (the host keeps none). A, on review ([793e6h1 What the user is shown goes through the host's log, and is not put in the prompt's context](./793e_implementation-plan.md#793e6h1-what-the-user-is-shown-goes-through-the-hosts-log-and-is-not-put-in-the-prompts-context) finding G3-1; [793c72 Where does the user see what automatic memory did?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c72-where-does-the-user-see-what-automatic-memory-did), [793c73 Is a line shown once, by a mark beside its record?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c73-is-a-line-shown-once-by-a-mark-beside-its-record), [793c74 Is what was delivered shown to the user?](./793c_open-questions.md#793c74-is-what-was-delivered-shown-to-the-user)).

## 793b108 A failure the mod reports is cut in its middle

The failure that the mod reports to the writing is cut in its middle, so that the step it names and the cause both stay, as the line that shows it is ([793b105 What is shown, and how](#793b105-what-is-shown-and-how)): a cut to its first 300 characters lost the cause behind a long path (the failed sweep of [793a2e1e A sweep that failed is shown with its reason](./793a_product-spec.md#793a2e1-uat-scenarios)). The writing cuts it: the mod sends the failure whole, and the writing writes every reason as one line, redacts it and only then cuts it, the order of [793d4j Redacting credentials](./793d_technical-spec.md#793d4j-redacting-credentials). ## 793b109 A headless turn that is not captured is recorded by the judgement
