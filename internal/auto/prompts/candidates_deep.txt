This is a deep recall, which also grows the vault. Beyond the verdicts:
- For each candidate note judged covered or near, list in its entry "chunks" the IDs of the passages among the
  candidates that evidence it, when there are any.
- A note that a newer candidate reverses is near, with fields that state what the newer candidate says, and not covered.
- For each candidate note judged near, give in its entry "fields" the fields to revise, so that the note states more
  completely the claim it already makes: the same subject, the same situation, the same principle. A revision sharpens
  the note's own claim and never adds a different claim: evidence for another claim is a lesson (below) or nothing, and
  no field is ever changed to hold a status, a plan, a branch, worktree or ticket name, a timestamp or an episode. Each
  field is a short string: for a fact note any of subject, predicate and object, for a feedback note any of behavior,
  impact and action, and for a runbook note any of situation, done_when and body. Revise from all the evidence, the
  later date winning a conflict. Never revise a note into a restatement of what it already says.
- In "lessons", list each lesson the evidence of the passages shows that no candidate note states and that is durable
  and atomic: still true and useful to a future task that has nothing to do with this one (no status, plan, decision
  about the work in hand, branch, worktree or ticket name, timestamp, finding about the incident of this session, or
  episode: the passages already hold the episodes), and one claim to a lesson. State it as a rule or a fact, and not
  the story, in the form in which it applies again, keeping the exact names, commands, flags and values it depends on;
  its situation is the kind of task that calls for it, with no ticket or change number unless the lesson is about it.
  Give each lesson "kind" ("feedback" for a rule about what to do or not to do, "fact" for something that is true),
  "slug" (kebab-case), "situation" (when it applies, as a retrieval cue), "covered" (null), "chunks" (the IDs of the
  passages that evidence it), and, for feedback, "behavior", "impact" and "action", and, for fact, "subject",
  "predicate" and "object", each a short string; and, when it corrects, narrows or refutes a candidate note,
  "supersedes" with that note's file name, a "type" of updates, narrows or refutes, and a "claim". A guess, a plan,
  chatter or a bare thanks is no lesson. Give one lesson for the principle of a set of passages, not one for each passage.
