Lesson learned: when dark leaf spotting on tomato leaves keeps spreading after switching to soaker hoses, it is early blight (a fungal disease), not splash-back -- at the first dark spots, remove the affected leaves and apply a copper fungicide within a day; soaker hoses alone do not fix it.
