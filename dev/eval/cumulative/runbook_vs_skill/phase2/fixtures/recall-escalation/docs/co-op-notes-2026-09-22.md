# Sprucebank Community Garden Co-op -- General Meeting Notes, Sept 22

Attendance: 11 members. Meeting opened 6:05pm at the tool shed. Treasurer reported dues are
current for 34 of 40 member households; five reminder emails will go out this week. The picnic
table by the north fence needs a new coat of sealant before the rain sets in -- volunteers can
sign up on the board by the mailboxes.

## Plot renewals
Renewal forms for next season are due by October 15th. Three plots (7, 12, 19) are still
unclaimed after the fall walkthrough -- email the plot coordinator if interested. The waiting
list has grown to nine names, so the board is discussing whether to split two of the larger
plots into halves next season to fit more members in.

## Shared equipment
The wheelbarrow with the flat tire has been patched. The seed-starting heat mats are back in
the tool shed after repair and available for checkout on the usual clipboard. The chipper-shredder
rental for the fall cleanup weekend is booked for the 28th; sign up for a two-hour slot if you
want first crack at the leaf piles.

## Board business, various
The board approved the annual budget for next year with no changes to plot fees. A new bike rack
was ordered for the front gate and should arrive within three weeks. Someone left a rake by the
compost bins again -- please bring tools back to the shed at the end of your visit. The library
box by the gate needs new weatherstripping before winter, and the board thanked the two members
who repainted the co-op sign last weekend. Parking along the north fence will be restriped next
month once the paint truck the county lent out is available. One purchasing-policy change passed
on a 9-2 vote and takes effect right away, folded into the same sustainable-sourcing push as last
year's switch away from plastic plant tags: the co-op's peat product line is discontinued
entirely, replaced going forward by a coir-based alternative, as part of moving every shared
purchase toward lower-impact materials. The newsletter will recap tonight's other votes in full
next week for anyone who missed the meeting, and the potluck sign-up sheet is still on the
clipboard by the door.

## Next meeting
October 20th, same time, same place. Bring pruning shears for the hedge workday afterward.
