Information learned: when in a trap/eval gate (gate.py, crowded_gate.py, or similar) returns RED on the current HEAD of a branch/change under review, and the natural next step is to apply that change's own prescribed fix for the regression, bisecting a gate regression to the change under review is not done_when-ready to ship until, in order: (1) the commit immediately preceding the change under review's relevant commits has been identified; (2) that preceding commit has been checked out; (3) the artifact under test has been rebuilt from that commit; (4) the identical gate (e.g. gate.py --tier smoke) has been re-run against that rebuilt artifact; (5) this verdict has been compared to the original RED verdict observed on the change under review's HEAD; (6) if the same failure reproduces on the pre-change commit, the change under review is NOT the cause and the pre-existing regression has been filed as its own separate issue for dedicated investigation; (7) if the failure does NOT reproduce on the pre-change commit, the change under review is confirmed as the cause and its prescribed fix applies; and (8) the original HEAD/branch has been restored and the artifact rebuilt before reporting results the regression has been correctly attributed as pre-existing (with a separate follow-up issue filed) or caused by the change under review, and the original HEAD/branch has been restored and rebuilt before reporting.
