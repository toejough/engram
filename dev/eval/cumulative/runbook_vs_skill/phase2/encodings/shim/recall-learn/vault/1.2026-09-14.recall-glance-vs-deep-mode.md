Recall runs in one of two modes, selected by the caller (the mode word is the invocation argument; absent → `deep`):

- `deep` (default). The full procedure — all 10 Step-1 phrases and the write side (coverage judgment's amend/learn, and the closing synthesis's persist step). It both *applies* memory to this decision **and** *grows the vault* (crystallizes, persists synthesis). Use it when the decision is weighty or irreversible, when you want the pass to also learn, or when in doubt.
- `glance` (opt-in, cheap — for firing often). A pass that is **read-only with respect to vault knowledge** (activation still bumps the used-notes recency metadata — that is kept, not a knowledge write). Run with **~3 phrases** (not 10) and **keep the read side** — reading candidates, applying the recency weight, activating used notes, the closing synthesis, and the re-entry query (when triggered) — but **skip the write side**: coverage judgment's amend/learn row, and the closing synthesis's persist step. Glance *applies* memory to this decision; it does **not** grow the vault's knowledge.

**Escalate `glance` → `deep` for recency-channel standards.** Glance reliably *surfaces* a recent-activity (Channel 2) item but does **not** elevate it to a requirement — measured: glance honors a recent-channel standard **0/5** where deep honors it **4/5**. So if your decision turns on **honoring a standard that surfaced in the recent-activity channel** (a "use X going forward" / "the new convention is Y" item in Channel 2), **switch to `deep`**. Glance is validated as deep-equivalent only for applying conventions, recency supersession within the matched set, and abduction/synthesis.

What glance skips, concretely:

- Step 1 (phrase generation): ~3 phrases instead of 10.
- The coverage-judgment procedure's write row (the amend/learn action) — still read and judge coverage, just do not write. See [[recall-note-synthesis-and-coverage]].
- The closing synthesis's persist step — do not hand a conclusion to [[8.2026-09-14.write-memory-compose-execute-verify]]. See [[recall-activation-and-closing-synthesis]].
- Everything else — the Step-0 upfront judgement, the sweep, running the query, reading candidates, applying the recency weight, activation, the closing synthesis itself, and the re-entry query (a query is a read) — runs the same in both modes.

Before producing the closing synthesis under `glance`, check for a load-bearing Channel 2 (recent-activity) standard. If your decision turns on honoring a recent convention, escalate to `deep` now, before synthesizing.
