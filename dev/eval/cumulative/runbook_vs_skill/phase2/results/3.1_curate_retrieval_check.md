**Setup.** Trial vault from `probe_phase2.py --task curate --arms R --shim-only --setup-only` (18 notes: the 9 fictional
beekeeping notes incl. 3 pending offers, the curate runbook, and the 8 recall/learn/write-memory runbooks that `--shim-only`
adds). Command shape: `engram query --lazy-chunks --chunks-dir <trial chunks> [--text T] --phrase P ...`, binary built from this
worktree's HEAD (whole-word trigger matching, commit b8e955a8); the installed `~/go/bin/engram` was also run on the trigger and
over-fire rows and gave identical results (so it already matches whole words). "Rank" = position of the curate runbook in
`items[]`; "provenance" = its `provenances` (`trigger` = a `triggers:` entry matched `--text`, ranks first and is exempt from the
relevance floor; `direct` = semantic top-k). Score is engram's similarity score (0 for a trigger-only hit).

**Caveat.** The trial vault is tiny (18 notes), so semantic ranks are optimistic and semantic over-fire is pessimistic
compared with the real vault (~1000 notes). The trigger channel does not depend on vault size.

## Reading

- **Trigger channel**: every text containing a trigger word (`curate`, `/curate`, `pending offers`, `pending offer`) surfaced the
  runbook at rank 1 with `trigger` provenance (8/8). Whole-word matching held: "accurate" and "curated" produced no trigger hit.
- **Semantic channel** (what a shim-only agent falls back on, and what harvested real phrases exercise): rank 1 or 2 in 6/6 real and
  shim-shaped forms. The 2 rank-2 rows lose to the seed vault's own summer-inspection note because the agent added the task's domain
  ("beekeeping hive management") to a phrase; a trigger-bearing `--text` restores rank 1.
- **Over-fire**: the bare word `curate` fires on non-vault senses ("curate a playlist", "what does curate mean"). This is the known
  trade of a bare-word trigger (glossary: candidate only). No other over-fire probe fired via `trigger`. Two probes that mention
  "offers" surfaced the runbook semantically in this 18-note vault; that needs a re-check against the production vault after
  promotion (task 4.1).
- **Not evidenced here**: whether a live agent runs the notice command and follows the runbook (tasks 3.2-3.4).
