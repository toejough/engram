Lesson learned: when implementing or changing date/time parsing, storage, or comparison in the Quillfeather static-site generator (front-matter dates), store and compare every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC in ISO-8601 (e.g. `2026-03-14T09:30:00Z`); accept both the `Z` and the explicit-offset forms and normalize to UTC; never fall back to local time.
