Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing dates or timestamps from page front matter in the Quillfeather static-site generator, Quillfeather stores and compares every front-matter date as UTC in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z); accept both Z and offset forms, normalize to UTC, and never use the host's local time zone.
