Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing dates/timestamps from page front matter in the Quillfeather static-site generator, treat every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z): accept both `Z` and numeric-offset forms, normalize to UTC, never use local time; test both forms.
