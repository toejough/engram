Lesson learned: when implementing or changing date handling for front-matter fields in the Quillfeather static-site generator, store and compare every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC, written in ISO-8601 (e.g. `2026-03-14T09:30:00Z`); normalize parsed dates to UTC and test both the `Z` and numeric-offset forms.
