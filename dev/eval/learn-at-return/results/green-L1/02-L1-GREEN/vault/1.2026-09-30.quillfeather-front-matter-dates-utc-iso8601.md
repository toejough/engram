Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates in the Quillfeather static-site generator, Quillfeather convention: every front-matter date is stored and compared as UTC, written in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z). Normalize parsed dates to UTC, never local time, and accept/test both the Z and numeric-offset forms..
