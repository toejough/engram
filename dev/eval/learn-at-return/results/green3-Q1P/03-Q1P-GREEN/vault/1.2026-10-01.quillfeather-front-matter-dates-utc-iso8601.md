Lesson learned: when implementing or changing date/time parsing or comparison for Quillfeather page front matter, Quillfeather stores and compares every front-matter date as UTC in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z); normalize parsed dates to UTC and accept both Z and numeric-offset forms; never rely on local time.
