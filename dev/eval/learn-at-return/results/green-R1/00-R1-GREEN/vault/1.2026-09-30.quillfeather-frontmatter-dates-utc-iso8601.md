Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates in the Quillfeather static-site generator, treat every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC and write it in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z). Accept both the Z and the explicit-offset forms, normalize them to UTC, and never fall back to local time..
