Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates in the Quillfeather static-site generator, treat every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC in ISO-8601 form (e.g. `2026-03-14T09:30:00Z`), accepting both `Z` and explicit-offset forms; never fall back to local time.
