Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates in the Quillfeather static-site generator, treat every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC ISO-8601 (e.g. `2026-03-14T09:30:00Z`): accept both `Z` and offset forms, normalize to UTC, never use local time.
