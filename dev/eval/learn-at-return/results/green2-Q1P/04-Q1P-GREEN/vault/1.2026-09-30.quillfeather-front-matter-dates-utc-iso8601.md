Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates/timestamps in the Quillfeather static-site generator, parse, store, and compare every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC, written in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z); accept both the Z and numeric-offset forms and normalize them to UTC; never use the local zone.
