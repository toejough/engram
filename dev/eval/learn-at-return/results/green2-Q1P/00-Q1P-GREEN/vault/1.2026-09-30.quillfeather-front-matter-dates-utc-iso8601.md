Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates in the Quillfeather static-site generator, store and compare every Quillfeather front-matter date as UTC in ISO-8601 form (e.g. `2026-03-14T09:30:00Z`); never use local time; test both the `Z` form and the offset form.
