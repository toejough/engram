Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates in the Quillfeather static-site generator, Quillfeather convention: store and compare every front-matter date as UTC, written in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z); accept both Z and numeric-offset forms and normalize to UTC; never depend on the host's local zone.
