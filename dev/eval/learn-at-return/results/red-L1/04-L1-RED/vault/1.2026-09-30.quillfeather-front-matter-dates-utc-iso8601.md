Lesson learned: when parsing, storing, or comparing front-matter dates in the Quillfeather static-site generator, Quillfeather stores and compares every front-matter date as UTC, written in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z); parse both Z and numeric-offset forms and normalize to UTC — never use the local time zone.
