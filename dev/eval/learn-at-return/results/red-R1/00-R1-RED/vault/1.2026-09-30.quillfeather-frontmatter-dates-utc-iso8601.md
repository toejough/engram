Lesson learned: when implementing or changing front-matter date/time parsing or comparison in the Quillfeather static-site generator, always store and compare Quillfeather front-matter dates as UTC, written in ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-03-14T09:30:00Z); test both the Z and offset forms; never depend on the host's local time zone.
